# Ai PORTFOLIO

[Explore the live portfolio](https://ai-portfolio-kohl-beta.vercel.app/)

**Praveen Rathee — business analysis, forecasting, AI workflows and operational insight.**

Ai PORTFOLIO brings my professional experience and project work into one place. Each project has its own visual world and a story you can follow: the question I explored, my contribution, the decisions behind the work, what was produced, and what the evidence does—and does not—support.

The portfolio is designed to make the work understandable before asking you to inspect a repository, spreadsheet or report. Those original materials remain linked where they matter.

## Three substantial project stories

### Evidence Lane Plugin

A persistent project system for Codex, conceived, directed, designed, reviewed and published with AI-assisted engineering. Its story connects the continuity problem to public workflows, traceable sources, bounded work, result inspection and recovery. The portfolio distinguishes the published source release from operation-specific readiness.

[Product website](https://evidencelane.org/) · [GitHub repository](https://github.com/rathee000001/evidence_lane_plugin)

### Gold Nexus Alpha

An ongoing forecasting and decision-support study that connects market and macroeconomic data, quality checks, features, Academic ML and Deep ML branches, evaluation outputs and evidence-linked interpretation. The story includes the limitations that qualify predictive claims.

[Live project](https://nyit-gold-intelligence-2026-4qlw.vercel.app/) · [GitHub repository](https://github.com/rathee000001/nyit-gold-intelligence-2026)

### Hershey Supply Chain AI

An independent academic study of one chocolate bar, connecting ingredient research, public sources, modeled production and distribution, benchmark costs and retail observations. The deployed interface connects the source-processing and analytical work to supply-chain, cost and methodology views. This is not a Hershey-affiliated or internal company system.

[Live project website](https://hershey-supply-chain-ai.vercel.app/) · [GitHub repository](https://github.com/rathee000001/hershey-supply-chain-ai)
