# Praveen Rathee — AI Portfolio

A portfolio of business analysis, forecasting, AI workflows and operational insight. Explore the original project worlds, follow each story, inspect its methods and limitations, and open the linked work.

**[Open the portfolio](https://ai-portfolio-kohl-beta.vercel.app/)**

## Explore the work

- [Evidence Lane Plugin](https://ai-portfolio-kohl-beta.vercel.app/projects/evidence-lane-plugin) — Long-running AI work can become fragmented: sources, decisions, unfinished tasks, tools and results end up in different places. I conceived, directed, designed, funded, reviewed and published Evidence Lane to keep that project context connected after the conversation moves on.
- [Evidence Lane App](https://ai-portfolio-kohl-beta.vercel.app/projects/evidence-lane-app) — The earlier Evidence Lane App explored a practical question: how can a local project retain its source material, proposed changes and accepted state when AI-assisted work evolves? It is the desktop prototype that preceded the current plugin system.
- [Gold Nexus Alpha](https://ai-portfolio-kohl-beta.vercel.app/projects/gold-nexus-alpha) — Gold Nexus Alpha began with gold-price forecasting and grew into a connected analytics and decision-support study. The work brings together data ingestion, quality checks, reusable features, multiple model families and explanations tied to saved outputs.
- [Gold.AI Regression](https://ai-portfolio-kohl-beta.vercel.app/projects/gold-regression) — The Gold.AI Regression Intelligence System turns a gold-price model into an inspectable analytical application. Inputs, training windows, coefficients, residuals, factor contributions and scenarios are available as parts of one story.
- [Gold Excel & Tableau](https://ai-portfolio-kohl-beta.vercel.app/projects/gold-excel-tableau) — The first gold forecasting prototype is an educational, spreadsheet-centered workflow. Source sheets, an assembled model table, coefficients, predictions, separate evaluation periods and Tableau comparison views make the calculation chain visible.
- [Chapters 4 Change](https://ai-portfolio-kohl-beta.vercel.app/projects/chapters-for-change) — Chapters 4 Change connects a campaign's purpose to the audiences, messages, channels and measures needed to act on it. The work combines a 15-page strategy presentation with a reviewable presentation-oriented website.
- [The Book Fairies](https://ai-portfolio-kohl-beta.vercel.app/projects/book-fairies) — The Book Fairies translates the related literacy-campaign strategy into an interactive web prototype. A visitor can discover the campaign, explore contribution choices, move into campus events and see simulated confirmation feedback.
- [DataCo Tableau](https://ai-portfolio-kohl-beta.vercel.app/projects/dataco) — The DataCo analysis connects demand, customer segments, product concentration and delivery performance in Tableau. Its purpose is to turn a large operational dataset into questions a business reviewer can follow.
- [SEC 10-K Analysis](https://ai-portfolio-kohl-beta.vercel.app/projects/sec-analysis) — This academic project structured a five-company, six-year comparison using manually collected SEC 10-K inputs. The work connects filing values to an Excel model, ratio families, charts, a written report and a group presentation.
- [Meta Case Analysis](https://ai-portfolio-kohl-beta.vercel.app/projects/meta-case) — The Meta case is an MBA strategy and organizational-behavior study based on public information. It examines how stakeholder interests, leadership, culture, control and decision processes interact with privacy and trust.
- [Hershey Supply Chain](https://ai-portfolio-kohl-beta.vercel.app/projects/hershey) — The Hershey study asks what public information can tell us about a HERSHEY’S 1.55 oz / 43 g milk chocolate bar: its ingredients, supplier context, modeled production and distribution, benchmark costs and the evidence behind those statements.

## Experience

[Business Analyst — Global Infrastructure & Advisory Services](https://ai-portfolio-kohl-beta.vercel.app/experience): structured multi-city property, transaction, commission, payment, maintenance and demand information into an Excel/Tableau operating view used by finance, operations and leadership for commercial decisions and follow-up.

## Using the site

Choose a world from Home, open a project, and move through its sections. Select the visible project objects to inspect their role. Skills and tools are searchable. The motion control pauses scene animations, and reduced-motion preferences are supported.

## Local development

Requires Node.js 24.

```sh
npm ci
npm run dev
```

The local site opens at `http://127.0.0.1:4173/`. Build with `npm run build`. Production is hosted on Vercel.

## Scope

Project stories distinguish prototypes, experimental systems and academic analyses from verified operational results. Evidence Lane Plugin and App are experimental; the Plugin’s broader development is on hold. Linked outputs and stated limitations belong to their respective projects.
